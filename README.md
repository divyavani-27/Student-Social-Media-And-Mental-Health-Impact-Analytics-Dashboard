# Student Lifestyle & Mental Health Analytics Dashboard

Power BI dashboard analyzing lifestyle habits, social media usage, and mental health for **5,000 students across 111 countries**.

## Overview
Explores how sleep, study time, physical activity, and social media use relate to student stress and mental health, across 5 report pages.

## Dashboards & Key Insights

**Executive Overview** — 5,000 students (4K UG, 918 Grad, 450 HS). Avg sleep 6.63 hrs, social media 5.08 hrs/day, mental health 6.23, stress 2.81. Instagram & TikTok are top platforms.

**Lifestyle Habits** — Avg sleep 6.75 hrs, study 2.94 hrs, activity 1.77. Graduates sleep most & study most; High Schoolers sleep least.

**Country Analytics** — 111 countries. Avg mental health 6.23, stress 2.81. Malta ranks highest (8.4); Venezuela lowest (3.3). Lower stress correlates with better mental health.

**Mental Health Analytics** — Graduates report highest mental health (6.4) vs Undergrad (6.2) and High School (5.9). Minimal gender gap (6.3 vs 6.2).

**Social Media Analytics** — Avg usage 5.08 hrs/day. Entertainment is top use case (2.6K), followed by Education and Networking. WhatsApp, TikTok, and Snapchat show highest engagement.
